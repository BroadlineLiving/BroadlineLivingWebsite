Privacy Policy

Broadline Living
Effective date: August 11, 2026

Broadline Living ("Broadline Living," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through broadlineliving.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you use our contact or inquiry forms, we may collect: your name, email address, phone number, and any details you provide about the property or unit you're inquiring about. If you communicate with us by phone, text, or email, we retain records of that communication to respond to you and service your inquiry or lease.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to inquiries about apartment availability, pricing, and tours
  • Coordinate leasing, move-in, and tenancy matters for current tenants
  • Send you text messages and emails related to your inquiry or tenancy, including scheduling, confirmations, and account or maintenance updates
  • Improve our website and services

Text Messaging (SMS) Consent

If you provide your phone number and opt in to receive text messages from Broadline Living — whether through a website form, in person with a leasing agent, or through a listing platform such as Furnished Finder — we will use that number to send you messages related to your inquiry, tour, lease, or tenancy. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP.

Mobile opt-in information and consent will not be shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. This information is used solely to facilitate SMS communications related to your relationship with Broadline Living, and is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own marketing.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (such as messaging or property-management platforms), and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services on our behalf. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You may ask us to correct or delete your personal information by contacting us at the email address below.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide to us, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to info@broadlineliving.com.
