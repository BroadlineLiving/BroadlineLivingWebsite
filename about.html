About Us

About Broadline Living

We make furnished city living effortless. We connect people with beautiful, move-in-ready homes and the personal service to match.

Our Story

Broadline Living was founded by industry professionals who know furnished rentals from both sides. We built this company to serve clients first, with the kind of personal, hands-on attention that only a small team can deliver, while also giving property owners the steady income and stewardship their homes deserve.

Our residents come from every walk of professional life. Executives on assignment, healthcare workers on rotation, production teams in the city for a shoot, consultants on extended engagements, academics visiting for a semester. What they all have in common is needing a home that's ready when they are, on terms that actually work for them.

We curate every apartment ourselves, partner directly with trusted landlords, and handle the details from first inquiry to move-in day. The result is a smoother experience for residents and a reliable, well-managed portfolio for owners.

Today we manage furnished homes across the city's most sought-after neighborhoods, with a focus on quality, transparency, and genuine service.
