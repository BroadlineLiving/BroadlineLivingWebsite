Our Story

Broadline Living was built around a simple idea: finding a furnished home in the city shouldn't be complicated. Too often, renters face endless listings, unclear terms, and properties that don't match their photos.

We curate every apartment ourselves, partner directly with trusted landlords, and handle the details from first inquiry to move-in day. The result is a smoother experience for residents and a reliable, well-managed portfolio for owners.

Today we manage furnished homes across the city's most sought-after neighborhoods, with a focus on quality, transparency, and genuine service.
